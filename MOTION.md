# MOTION.md — Ubuntu Circle

Read this in full before you design, generate or animate anything for me.
It was built from my Ubuntu Circle 30-day card series (30 still cards,
1080 × 1080, lossless PNG). Every hex code and size below was measured
from those files, not estimated. Anything the cards cannot show is
marked ASK ME. Stop and ask; do not fill the gap yourself.

The cards hold **two looks**. Know which one you are making.

- **Look A: Witness.** Days 1–7, tagged #StopFemicide. Flat dark green,
  upright serif, teal accents, a helpline in the footer. For advocacy
  about gender-based violence.
- **Look B: Circle.** Days 8–30. Softly lit green, italic serif, the
  closing phrase in gold, faint concentric rings, my name in the footer.
  For reflection and leadership content.

Which look goes with which kind of video: **ASK ME.**

The fonts were identified by matching letter shapes and measured widths
against the real fonts (within 1%). Whether these are the fonts you
chose, or stand-ins for something else: **ASK ME.**

Quote length on the cards ranges from 1 to 6 lines. Gold always covers
the *last* clause or phrase, never the opening, and can run across a
line break.

### Layout (1080 × 1080)

- **Top stripe:** 7 px tall, full width. Green 0–355, gold 356–539,
  red 540–722, blue 723–863, white 864–1080.
- **Left border:** 6 px, full height below the stripe. Teal (A) or gold (B).
- **Header:** label centred, about 45 px from the top. A 1 px rule under
  it, 400 px wide (x 340–740) at y 64. Deep teal (A) or gold (B).
- **Quote:** centred on the frame, centred text, block centred near y 505.
- **Divider:** 180 px wide, 2 px tall, centred, about 50 px below the
  quote's last line. Deep teal (A) or dark gold (B).
- **Rings (B only):** concentric circles centred behind the quote,
  1–2 px lines, about 70–80 px apart, in Ring green.
- **Footer:** 1 px deep-teal rule at y 1016, then a 63 px bar in Footer green.
- Everything else stays empty.

Other aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16): **ASK ME.**

## 5. Texture and finish

- Flat and clean. No grain, noise, paper texture or halftone.
- No drop shadows, glows, outlines or 3D on type.
- Look A: one flat background colour.
- Look B: a soft vertical gradient, lightest behind the quote and darker
  at the top and bottom edges, plus the faint rings.
- Hairlines are 1 px, the divider 2 px. No thick rules.
- No photos, illustrations, icons or logos appear on any card.
- Whether a photo or logo may ever be added: **ASK ME**

## 6. Never

1. **Never use a colour that is not in section 1.** And never use the
   flag colours anywhere except the top stripe.
2. **Never put more than one quote on screen,** and never crowd it.
   The quote sits alone in the centre with empty space around it.
3. **Never set the quote in a sans-serif,** or off-centre, or in any
   colour but cream and gold. Sans-serif is for the header and footer only.
4. **Never gild anything but the closing phrase.** Gold in the quote
   marks the last clause. It never marks the opening, a single random
   word, or the whole quote.
5. **Never drop the frame furniture:** the flag stripe, the left border,
   the header label and rule, and the footer bar. In Look A, the
   helpline `GBV Command Centre 0800 428 428 · 24 hours` must be on
   screen, readable, the whole time.

These come from the design of the cards. What your *motion* must never
do (for example: bounce, spin, flash, glitch, shake): **ASK ME.**
